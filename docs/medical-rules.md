# Adrift medical rules

First Aid keeps a survivor alive. Surgery repairs them. Nothing else turns a Major or Deadly injury into a Minor one.

## Injuries at a glance

| Severity | Penalty | Until Surgery | Clock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minor | −1d6 to the rolls it lists | — | Heals on a Rest once Treated |
| Major | −2d6 to the rolls it lists | Its condition, Bleeding when it lands, Pain | Festers into Deadly after 3 days |
| Deadly | −2d6 to the rolls it lists | Bleeding when it lands, Pain | Dies after four segments, or is a permanent loss |

Every Untreated injury costs 1 HP at End of Day, whatever its severity.

## What leaving a wound unoperated costs

- **Pain.** Every Major or Deadly injury that hasn't had Surgery costs 1 Maximum Energy. The point returns the moment the Surgery succeeds. First Aid doesn't ease it.
- **Festering.** A Major injury left 3 days without Surgery, counting the day it happened, festers at that End of Day. It becomes a Deadly injury, and its four-segment Surgery window opens from the next Morning. A wound taken on day 1 festers at the End of Day on day 3.
- **The Deadly clock.** A Deadly injury that demands Surgery kills the character once its window passes: the segment it happened in and the three after it, rolling into the next day if it must.

## First Aid: stabilise

A free action that uses one medkit and is always a Skilled roll. The patient's player has to agree.

- The first 6 treats an Untreated injury of any severity. Treated injuries stop costing HP at End of Day.
- Treating a Deadly injury buys one more segment on its Surgery deadline.
- Every other 6 heals 1 HP or clears a condition, such as Bleeding.
- It never makes a Major or Deadly injury Minor, and it doesn't stop Pain or Festering.

## Surgery: repair

A Crisis task on one named Major or Deadly injury. It costs the surgeon 3 Energy. The patient spends that segment on the table and takes no Task of their own. Nobody operates on themselves.

**Requirements.** The only requirement is Surgical Tools, either in the surgeon's pack or, while aboard, in ship storage. Each Surgery spends one use of the best set to hand, success or fail.

| Tools | Bonus | Energy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Improvised Surgical Tools | — | 3 |
| Surgical Tools | — | 3 |
| Enhanced Surgical Tools | +3d6 | 0 |

**The theatre.** The Medical Bay sets the penalty and how far the patient's HP drops when the operation begins. The drop only applies if they're above it, and never takes them below 1 HP. The Medical Bay only counts aboard. A working Mobile Surgical Cradle counts as a Field Setup wherever its mech is.

| Where | Roll | HP on success | HP on failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No Medical Bay, or Base Setup | −3d6 | ½ Max HP | ½ Max HP |
| Field Setup, or a Mobile Surgical Cradle | −2d6 | ½ Max HP | ½ Max HP |
| Operational | −1d6 | ¾ Max HP | ½ Max HP |
| Sterile Suite | — | ¾ Max HP | ¾ Max HP |
| Advanced Care | — | No drop | No drop |

A successful **Prep for Surgery** on the patient (Routine, First Aid 7, Sterile Suite) adds +2d6 to their Surgery later that day.

**Outcomes.**

- **Success:** the injury becomes a Treated Minor injury. Its condition ends, its Pain ends, and its deadline is cleared. From then on it heals on a Rest like any Minor injury, unless it's a permanent loss.
- **Strong Result:** the patient also regains 1 HP.
- **Failure:** the injury is exactly as it was. The Energy, the tool use and the HP drop are spent anyway, so a second attempt starts from lower HP.

## Base Energy

Every human's base Maximum Energy is 4, up from 3. Constitution adds to it as before. Pain comes off this total.

## In the app

- Plan a Surgery task, then pick the patient and the injury. The app enforces the tools and the theatre. When the task resolves, it spends the tools, drops the HP and applies the result.
- First Aid's deadline extension, Pain and Festering all apply on their own. The character sheet shows Pain on the Energy meter and counts down the nights a Major injury has left.
- The GM keeps the manual Surgery button on an injury, as an override.
