# Using the Adrift Companion app

The Adrift Companion is a web app that tracks a crew's survival: characters, ship, storage, tasks, and days. It saves in the browser, and a campaign can be shared so everyone edits it live.

## Playing together
- The GM opens **Play together** in the header and creates a shared campaign. It gets a six-character code.
- Players open **Play together**, enter the code, press **Find**, and join. Each player then claims their character on the Crew tab ("You now play …").
- In a shared campaign, players edit only their own character, items in ship storage or their own inventory, and their own planned tasks. Projects are the GM's: crew add Progress by resolving a Work on a project task. The GM can change anything.
- **New campaign** leaves the shared campaign and starts a fresh local one; it never overwrites the shared game.

## The day
- The header shows the day and segment (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night). **Next segment** advances it.
- A segment happens all at once, so what a task makes for the ship arrives when the segment turns. The Tasks tab lists everything in hand under **Landing at …**, and the room or Project it belongs to is marked on the Ship tab. What a task does to a person — Energy, HP, Stress, Pressure, injuries, implants, an Assist's dice — applies straight away.
- **End day** opens the End of Day check: who ate, drank, and rested, then applies starvation, dehydration, sleep, and untreated-injury effects, resets Pressure to the Stress floor, grows crops, collects solar Fuel, spends a day of the grid's Power Cell charge, and starts the next Morning.

## Tabs
- **Overview**: the crew and ship at a glance, with alerts (down, dehydrated, and so on).
- **Actions**: plan each survivor's task for the current segment. **Plan a task** picks a shipboard task; **Push for an extra task** costs 1 Energy and 1 Pressure. Players roll and resolve their own tasks with **Resolve**; Routine tasks succeed without a roll. Some tasks need the GM's permission (**Ask the GM for permission**).
- **Crew**: character sheets. HP, Energy, Willpower, Armor, Pressure and Stress sit at the top, then attributes, skills, survival, conditions, injuries, afflictions, abilities, cybernetics, and inventory. **New survivor** creates a character.
- **Ship**: rooms and their conditions, power, Hydroponics crops, projects (**New project**, including Install Module and Upgrade Module), **Build facility**, and **Take a Hit** for hull damage. Each room also has a spare slot beside its module for one set of Makeshift Solar Panels or one set of defenses; panels collect 1 Fuel Unit a day at End of Day.
- **Mechs**: mech frames, modules, repairs, deployment, and players' mech requests to the GM.
- **Storage**: ship storage, sorted alphabetically, and **Add item**.
- **Map**: grid maps for expeditions and star charts for navigation.
- **Log**: everything that has happened, newest first.

## On a character sheet
- **Roll** opens the dice roller for any skill or attribute (attribute + skill dice, plus Pressure dice; each 6 is a success).
- **Damage** applies incoming damage: Armor absorbs 1 and loses 1 point per hit, losing 2+ HP raises Pressure (once per round), and losing 3+ HP from one hit or dropping to 0 HP causes an Injury roll. Enter the two d6 (Severity, then Location); the app shows the injury and warns when the cap or a duplicate makes it worse.
- **Injuries** apply their dice penalty to matching rolls automatically. Major and Deadly injuries start Bleeding and add their condition; Deadly ones show a Surgery deadline. **Surgery** records a successful operation, turning the injury into a Treated Minor one. Injuries marked **old table** come from the previous 60-entry table and work as before; **Convert** swaps one for the closest new injury, keeping its state.
- **First Aid** is a free action, any time, no Energy, but it uses up a medkit (carried or from ship storage). Pick any living patient, and optionally an Untreated injury (any severity) to treat. The kit heals its HP and adds its dice; treating takes the first 6, and every other 6 heals 1 HP or clears a condition you ticked. Giving First Aid to another player's character sends them an offer they Accept or Decline.
- The GM's **Rest** button: +1 HP, restores Energy, lowers Pressure, and rolls to heal the oldest Treated Minor injury. A character who had no Food or Water in the last day gets none of that (the Rest only counts as sleep). Sleep Disruption, Compulsive Distraction, and the old table's Minor Shock and Internal Bleeding change what a Rest gives, and the log says how.
- **Install cybernetic** installs implants (the GM can force-install any cybernetic).
